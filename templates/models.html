How to read these numbers

Accuracy

The share of all cases predicted correctly.

Sensitivity (recall)

The share of people with the condition that the model correctly flags. High sensitivity matters for screening.

Specificity

The share of people without the condition that the model correctly clears.

ROC-AUC

How well the model ranks higher-risk cases above lower-risk ones. 1.0 is perfect; 0.5 is guessing.

These models are trained on public study data from a limited group of patients. Performance on your own population may differ, and none of the models is clinically validated.
